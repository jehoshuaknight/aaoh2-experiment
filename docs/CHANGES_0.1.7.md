# 0.1.7: the ethnicity map in game

Press **F6** to cycle map styles: Realistic → Strategic → **Peoples of 2240** → Holo Table. Peoples of 2240 replaces Satellite Night on Earth and Mars. Bodies without provinces still get Satellite Night under that style.

- Every people in its own colour, over gentle relief. Mixed provinces show their peoples interleaved in patches, by their shares.
- Region borders are bold ink and province borders thin ink, so you can see which side is which. The game's own place names stay on top.
- Seas are dark, with the coastal shelf and depth lines.

## How it works without engine changes
- `shaders/ethnicity_tables.slang` is generated by `tools/eth_tables.py`. It holds every province's centre and top three peoples with their shares, for Earth (480) and Mars (200), as `switch` lookups. Constant arrays would become per-pixel copies on the GPU; switches don't.
- The shader reads each pixel's region exactly from the engine's province data. Inside the region it picks the nearest province centre, with a noisy edge. Tested against the real province map, this is the right province on 84 % of Earth's land and 87 % of Mars's. The misses are near province edges inside a region, where neighbours have similar peoples.
- The style's name on screen was changed in `astra_client.exe`, by a same-length text replacement: "Satellite Night" → "Peoples of 2240", and "Map: Satellite Night [F6]" → "Map: Peoples of 2240 [F6]". No code was changed.

To change the map after editing the ethnicity data:

```
python3 tools/eth_tables.py <AstraNovum> shaders/ethnicity_tables.slang
slangc shaders/mesh.slang fragmentLit bin/game/shaders/mesh.lit.frag.spv shaders
```

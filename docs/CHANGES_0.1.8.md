# 0.1.8: 100 fusion peoples

Earth now has 352 peoples: 252 real ones, plus **100 fusion peoples of 2240**. Each fusion people was born from two parent cultures meeting in cities, launch towns and climate-migration corridors (`tools/groups_fusion.py`).

| Where | Fusion peoples | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| China | 16 | Amurese (Russian + Han), Pudong Arcologists (Wu + Japanese), Silk Corridor Uyghan, Qinghai Salt Nomads |
| United States | 16 | Tejano-Texians, Cascadian Nikkei-Yankees, Bay Silicon Desi, Rocky Mountain Launch Clans, Floridian Exiles |
| Brazil | 15 | Nipo-Paulistas, Neo-Yoruba Bahians, Amazon Bio-Caboclos, Planalto Orbital Families |
| Russia | 16 | Volga Tatar-Rus, Primorye Koryo-Rus, Norilsk Dome Clans, Kamchatka Pacific Mix |
| Elsewhere | 37 | Ibadan Pan-Africans, Bengaluru Neo-Kannadigas, Berlin Turco-Germans, Koryo-Saram, Tusán, Porteño Exiles |

Peoples whose city drowned under the +70 m sea were moved inland and named as exiles (Florida, Rio, Buenos Aires, Dakar).

## More natural
- Fusion peoples are urban: they are strong where the night lights are bright and faint in the country.
- Cities mix more finely, on the baked map and in game: the mosaic grows finer and busier with the lights.
- Each fusion people's colour starts as a blend of its two parents' colours, kept apart from its neighbours by the contrast pass.
- In game the map now shows up to **five** peoples per province (it was three). 96 of the 100 fusion peoples appear in game, in 125 provinces. All 100 are in the data files and the web atlas.
